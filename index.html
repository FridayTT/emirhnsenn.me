About Me

Hi, I'm
Emirhan Sen

A 22-year-old athlete from Istanbul, Turkey who has proudly represented Turkey abroad and achieved numerous successes both domestically and internationally.

I started squash at age 5 — my father was my first coach. By 10, I was competing in European Squash Federation (ESF) tournaments across 12 years of international competition. At my peak, I reached World No. 2 in the BU13 category.

Currently studying at St. Lawrence University (USA), playing No. 3 on the varsity Men's Squash team while maintaining strong academic performance.

My goal is to go professional and reach the world top ranking — I train with discipline, focus, and full commitment to achieve it.
