About Me

Hi, I'm
Emirhan Sen

A 22-year-old athlete who has proudly represented Turkey abroad and achieved numerous successes both domestically and internationally.

I have been passionately involved in squash for 17 years, including 12 years of competing in European tournaments. Currently, I am pursuing my studies at St. Lawrence University in the United States, where I continue to excel in squash while balancing my academic responsibilities.

My goal is to become a professional and reach the top ranking — I train with discipline, focus, and full commitment to achieve it.
